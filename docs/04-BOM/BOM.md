## Overview
The Bill of Materials (BOM) for this subsystem includes all electronic, mechanical, and supporting components required to assemble the camera subsystem PCB. This includes active components such as the ESP32-S3 microcontroller and OV2640 camera sensor, passive components such as resistors and capacitors, power components including voltage regulators, and mechanical components such as connectors and mounting hardware.

Each component entry includes key information such as the manufacturer part number, quantity, reference designator, package type, the chosen supplier, and links to each component's datasheet and product page. This ensures that all components can be accurately sourced and assembled without ambiguity.
